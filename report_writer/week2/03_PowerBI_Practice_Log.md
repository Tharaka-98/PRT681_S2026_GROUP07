# Week 2 Power BI Practice Log

## Dataset idea
IT support tickets with category, priority, status, team and resolution hours.

## Visuals practised
- Card: total ticket count
- Column chart: tickets by category
- Donut chart: open vs closed status
- Bar chart: tickets by assigned team
- Table: high-priority unresolved tickets
- Slicer: priority

## What I noticed
A dashboard becomes easier to read when every visual answers a different question. Repeating the same metric in several chart types adds clutter rather than insight.
